# Theme Planner

The **Theme Planner** (under **Appearance → Theme Planner**) is the fastest way to give the site your church's colors. Instead of setting a dozen colors one at a time, you pick your main color and the planner works out the rest of the palette for you.

Only administrators can see this page.

## What it does

Nearly everything on the site is colored from three color families:

- **Primary** — your main brand color. Used for the header, buttons, links, and the footer.
- **Secondary** — a supporting color, used for things like the breadcrumb bar.
- **Accent** — a highlight color, used for hover states and small details.

Primary and Secondary each have four shades, from darkest to lightest. The planner asks you for the darkest shade of each and generates the three lighter shades automatically, so the whole ramp stays consistent.

## Using the planner

1. Go to **Appearance → Theme Planner**.
2. Under **Primary**, click the **Primary color** swatch and choose your church's main color. The four tiles beneath it — *Primary 100* through *Primary 400* — update straight away so you can see the shades you are getting.
3. Look at the suggestions box below. Pick a **Suggestion type** and the two small circles show a suggested **Secondary** and **Accent** color drawn from standard color theory. The three types are described below.
4. Press **Swap** if you would rather have the two suggestions the other way round. This only changes the preview.
5. Press **Apply to Secondary & Accent** to accept the suggestions. The Secondary and Accent pickers further down the page jump to those colors, and Secondary's lighter shades regenerate.
6. If you already know the colors you want, ignore the suggestions entirely and set the **Secondary color** and **Accent color** pickers by hand.
7. Click **Save Colors**. You will see a **Colors saved.** confirmation.

### The suggestion types

- **Split complementary** — two colors from opposite the primary on the color wheel. The safest, most balanced choice, and the default.
- **Triadic** — two colors evenly spaced around the wheel from the primary. Bolder and more contrasting.
- **Analogous** — two colors sitting close either side of the primary. Quiet and harmonious, good for a restrained look.

## What Save Colors changes

Saving writes the nine colors — four Primary shades, four Secondary shades, and Accent — into exactly the same places that **Appearance → Customize → Site Colors** edits. The planner and the Customizer are two ways into one palette, so you can start in the planner and then fine-tune a single shade in the Customizer afterwards.

Saving here also rebuilds the site's stylesheet immediately, so visitors who are not logged in see the new colors right away.

## What it does not change

- **The neutral colors.** Your whites, off-white, greys, black, and the accessibility outline color are left alone. Set those in **Site Colors** if you need to.
- **Which color goes where.** *Site Colors* has a second group of settings that decide which palette color each part of the site uses — header background, menu text, card backgrounds, and so on. Those keep pointing at the same palette slots, which is why re-planning your colors flows through the whole site without you touching them again.

There is no live preview of the site on this page and no undo, so it is worth exporting your settings first if you are replacing a palette you might want back. See the *Theme Settings* page for the export tool.
