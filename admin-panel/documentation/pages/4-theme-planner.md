# Theme Planner

The **Theme Planner** is a quick way to set your site's three main brand colors without working through the full color list in the Customizer. Open it from **Appearance → Theme Planner**.

Pick your primary color, let the planner suggest a matching secondary and accent, and save. The planner works out all the lighter shades for you.

## Why use it instead of Site Colors

The *Site Colors* section of the Customizer gives you every color slot individually — eighteen of them. That is the right tool when you want precise control.

The Theme Planner is the shortcut. It sets just the colors your brand is built on and fills in the supporting shades automatically, so a new site can look finished in a minute or two. You can always open *Site Colors* afterwards to fine-tune anything the planner set.

## Setting your colors

### 1. Choose your primary color

At the top of the page, click the **Primary color** swatch and pick your church's main brand color. This is the color used for your header background, headings, and most links.

As soon as you choose it, the row of swatches beneath updates. You will see four shades — **Primary 100** through **Primary 400**. The color you picked becomes *Primary 100* (the darkest), and the three lighter shades are generated from it automatically. You do not set those by hand.

### 2. Use the suggestions for secondary and accent

Below the primary swatches is a suggestion box that proposes a matching **Secondary** and **Accent** color based on standard color-wheel relationships. Choose a **Suggestion type**:

- **Split complementary** — the default. Two colors from opposite the primary on the color wheel. A strong, confident contrast that still looks deliberate.
- **Triadic** — three colors evenly spaced around the wheel. Bolder and more colorful.
- **Analogous** — colors sitting next to the primary on the wheel. A gentle, harmonious look.

Two round preview swatches show what each suggestion would give you, labelled **→ Secondary** and **→ Accent**.

- Click **Swap** to flip them, so the suggestion that was going to be your secondary becomes your accent instead.
- Click **Apply to Secondary & Accent** to accept them. The planner fills in both colors and regenerates their shades.

The suggestions are only ever previews until you click *Apply* — looking through the options never changes anything.

### 3. Adjust by hand if you want

You are not bound to the suggestions. Each family has its own picker:

- **Secondary color** — used for softer backgrounds and supporting areas such as the breadcrumb bar.
- **Accent color** — a single color with no lighter shades, used for highlights such as link hover states.

Pick either one directly and the planner regenerates its shades the same way it does for the primary.

### 4. Save

Click **Save Colors**. You will see a *Colors saved.* confirmation, and the new colors apply across the whole site right away — for visitors as well as for you.

## What the planner changes, and what it does not

The planner writes to the same color settings that **Appearance → Customize → Site Colors** uses. Specifically it sets:

- Your four **primary** shades.
- Your four **secondary** shades.
- Your **accent** color.

It does not touch anything else. Your whites, greys, black, and outline colors stay as they are, and so does every choice about *which* color is used *where* — header background, button colors, footer text, and so on. Those stay in *Site Colors*, and because the planner updates the underlying shades, the areas using them simply pick up the new colors.

> Tip: The planner is the fastest way to re-brand an existing site. Change the primary color, apply fresh suggestions, save, and every area that referenced those shades updates at once — no need to revisit each setting.
